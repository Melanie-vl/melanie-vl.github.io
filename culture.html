Talent programme for young artists

Length

A three-month programme for young artists aged 17 to 27. Each participant developed a new artwork, supported by a series of workshops, including one given by the Frans Hals Museum.

I mentored the artists for the full three months. I was their point of contact and helped them plan and develop their work.
