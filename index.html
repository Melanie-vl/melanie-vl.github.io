About me

I'm an event producer with a strong interest in art and culture. Over the past few years I have worked on festivals, conferences, games, neighbourhood events and a three-month programme for young artists.

I keep track of budgets, schedules and scripts, I'm the point of contact for artists and partners, and I like to be on the floor when the event happens. My goal is to create programmes where visitors feel welcome, and that stay with them.

  • Production
  • Budget management
  • Artist and partner liaison
  • Mentoring
  • Hosting
  • Concept development
  • Sponsorship
  • Educational programmes
