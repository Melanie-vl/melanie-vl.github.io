Bingo & Bubbels

Connecting residents and local businesses

When
28 May 2025
Role
Research, sponsorship, promotion, permits

A street bingo to connect residents and local businesses in the neighbourhood.

I researched how to strengthen that connection and turned the findings into the approach. I secured sponsors for the prizes and drinks, arranged the permits and handled promotion by inviting residents personally and handing out flyers.

DJYOURSTRESSAWAY

DJ workshop in a bicycle parking facility

When
3 April 2025
Role
Research, concept development, sponsorship

A DJ workshop in a bicycle parking facility, designed to make the facility more visible and improve the atmosphere.

I researched the effect of music on wellbeing and stress, and used it as the basis for a low-threshold, energetic concept. I also secured sponsors for sound and lighting.
