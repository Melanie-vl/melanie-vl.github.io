Parksessies 2025

Cultural music festival

When
13, 20 and 27 August 2025
Role
Finance, sponsorship, production, social media

Parksessies is a cultural music festival in the park with live music, DJs, art and activities for children.

I managed the budget and financial overviews, and recruited and maintained sponsors. I ran the festival's Instagram, planning and creating content to reach a wider audience. I helped shape the concept and atmosphere, contributed to the scripts and set up the production books. During the festival days I coordinated people and materials.

Sound of Alkmaar

Benefit concert for Muziekids

When
6 June 2024
Role
Finance, stakeholder management, sponsorship, team lead

Sound of Alkmaar was a benefit concert raising money for Muziekids. I played a central role in the organisation. I managed the finances, was the point of contact for all parties involved, and secured sponsors and partners to make the concert financially possible.

I also led the team and kept the planning on track.
