Twist the Theory

A card game for interactive teaching

When
17 December 2025
Role
Research, concept development, game design

Twist the Theory is a card game that helps teachers make their lessons more interactive. Students draw a card and explain a theory in a creative way, for example as a podcast, a poem or a parody. This makes them process the material actively, so they remember it longer.

I researched learning techniques and how people retain knowledge. With those insights, my team and I developed the concept through a series of creative sessions into a playable card game, which students tested during a play session.

Wannadobetter

A debate game about Dutch politics

When
24 November 2025
Role
Concept development, research, educational design

Wannadobetter is a debate and role-playing game that makes Dutch politics accessible to everyone. In three hours, players go through the full political process, from election campaigns to passing a bill in the Senate. Along the way they hold meetings, negotiate and debate.

I researched Dutch politics, game design and educational methods, and turned that research into the game format. I wrote a production book and two manuals, one for players and one for organisers, so anyone in the Netherlands can run the game themselves.
