Healthy Worklife

B2B networking event

When
20 January 2025
Role
Budget management, team lead, logistics

Healthy Worklife was a free networking event to connect new partners with Inholland Amsterdam, featuring guest speakers, workshops, catering and team activities.

I managed the budget and led a team. I made sure the other teams had enough budget for speakers, catering and workshops, while keeping the event free for visitors. On the evening itself I checked the venue and materials and monitored the flow of visitors.
